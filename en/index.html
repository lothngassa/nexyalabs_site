Features

Everything you need, in one place.

One app replaces a dozen tools, without asking you to juggle between them.

Multi-model chat

NEXYA draws on several leading AI models and picks the right one for each question.

AI memory

It remembers what matters from one chat to the next. You see and delete what it knows.

Voice

Dictate your question and listen to the answer, hands-free, with six voices to choose from.

Vision

Snap a document, an exercise or an object: NEXYA analyses it and explains it.

Images

Create original visuals from a simple description, clearly labelled as AI-generated.

Professional documents

Real PDF and Word files, formatted and ready to send: letters, reports, minutes.

Projects

Drop your files into a project and NEXYA uses them to answer you.

Planner

Ask for a reminder in the chat. NEXYA schedules it and notifies you on time.

Ready-to-send drafts

Email, WhatsApp, SMS or LinkedIn post: NEXYA writes it, you send it in one tap.
